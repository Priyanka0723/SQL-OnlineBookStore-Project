# Key Business Insights

## 1. Overall Store Performance

- The bookstore contains **500 books**, **500 customers**, and **500 orders**.
- A total of **2,697 books** were sold across the recorded orders.
- The bookstore generated approximately **$75,628.66 in total revenue**.
- The average order value was approximately **$151.26**.

## 2. Customer Activity

- Out of 500 registered customers, **307 customers placed at least one order**.
- This means a significant portion of registered customers did not place an order during the analyzed period.
- Customer activity can therefore be explored further to identify high-value customers and inactive customers.

## 3. Book Performance

- Out of 500 books in the catalog, **317 books were ordered at least once**.
- **183 books had no recorded orders**, indicating that a considerable portion of the catalog did not generate sales during the analyzed period.
- These books could be investigated further to understand whether pricing, genre, availability, or customer demand contributed to their low performance.

## 4. Genre Performance

- **Romance generated the highest revenue**, contributing approximately **$13,086.98**.
- **Mystery recorded the highest sales volume**, with **504 books sold**.
- Science Fiction also performed strongly, with **447 books sold** and approximately **$11,770.51 in revenue**.
- Fiction had the lowest sales volume among the major genres, with **225 books sold** and approximately **$7,271.22 in revenue**.

### Key observation

The genre with the highest number of books sold does not necessarily generate the highest revenue. Romance generated more revenue than Mystery despite selling fewer books.

This shows that **sales volume and revenue are two different performance measures**.

## 5. Yearly Performance

- **2023** recorded **256 orders**, with **1,386 books sold** and approximately **$36,339.97 in revenue**.
- **2024** recorded fewer orders than 2023, with **228 orders**, but generated slightly higher revenue of approximately **$36,775.33**.
- This indicates that the bookstore generated **higher revenue in 2024 despite having fewer orders**.

### Key observation

The increase in revenue despite fewer orders suggests that the **average value of orders in 2024 was higher** than in 2023.

## 6. 2022 Performance

- 2022 had only **16 recorded orders**, with **79 books sold**.
- Revenue during this period was approximately **$2,513.36**.
- Compared with 2023 and 2024, 2022 represents a much smaller portion of the overall business activity.

## 7. Revenue and Sales Volume

The analysis shows that **revenue and quantity sold should be analyzed separately**.

For example:

- Mystery → highest quantity sold
- Romance → highest revenue

This distinction is useful for identifying both **high-demand genres** and **high-revenue genres**.

## 8. International Customer Base

- The customer dataset contains customers from **215 different countries**.
- Customers are spread across **489 cities**.
- This indicates that the bookstore has a highly geographically diverse customer base.

This provides an opportunity for further analysis of revenue and customer activity by country and city.

## 9. Product Catalog Opportunity

- With **183 books having no recorded orders**, the bookstore has a large group of products that could potentially be investigated for poor performance.
- These books could be analyzed based on genre, price, publication year, and stock levels.
- Identifying underperforming books could help the business make better decisions about promotions, pricing, inventory, or catalog management.

## 10. Overall Business Conclusion

The SQL analysis shows that the online bookstore generated approximately **$75.6K in revenue from 2,697 books sold across 500 orders**.

The analysis also reveals several important differences between customer activity, sales volume, and revenue. Romance was the highest-revenue genre, while Mystery had the highest sales volume. Furthermore, 2024 generated slightly more revenue than 2023 despite having fewer orders.

The presence of **183 books with no recorded sales** and **193 registered customers without orders** also highlights potential areas for further investigation.

Overall, the analysis demonstrates how SQL can be used to transform raw bookstore data into **actionable business insights related to sales, customers, products, revenue, and business performance**.
